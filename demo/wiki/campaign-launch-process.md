# Campaign launch process

## Approvals
Every new campaign needs a brief signed off by the CVM lead and by the channel owner.
Legal approval is required when the offer changes the tariff price.

## Timeline
The brief is due 10 working days before launch. Targeting lists are frozen 2 days before launch.
After the freeze nobody edits the list; changes go into the next wave.

## Holdout
Every campaign keeps a control group, see [[control-group-policy]].
