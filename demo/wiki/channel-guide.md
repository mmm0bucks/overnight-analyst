# Channel guide

## SMS
Highest reach, cheapest per contact. Opt-out rate is the highest of all channels (about 0.8% per send).
Messages are limited to 70 Cyrillic characters.

## Push
Only for customers with the mobile app. Low cost, low opt-out, but delivery is not guaranteed.

## Email
Lowest opt-out, slowest response. Good for long offers and family plans.
